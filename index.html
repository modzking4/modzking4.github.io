Sheet 04 — Method

How I work, solo, from brief to live.

  1. 01

    Understand the problem

    Before writing anything, I get clear on what the app or site actually needs to do for the people using it day to day — not just the feature list I've been handed.

  2. 02

    Build in the open

    I work in short, visible increments — sharing progress builds early rather than disappearing for weeks, so direction can change while it's still cheap to change it.

  3. 03

    Ship and support

    Getting it live is the milestone, not the finish line — I stay on for fixes, updates, and the inevitable edge case that only shows up once real users touch it.
