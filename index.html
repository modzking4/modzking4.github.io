Sheet 04 — Method

How a build actually moves.

  1. 01

    Define

    Speak to the client to get the real spec — what they need the site or app to do for their business, not just what they think they want.

  2. 02

    Design

    Keep the client inside the design stage so their ideas are actually heard and developed, with sign-off at each step before build starts.

  3. 03

    Build

    Build against the approved design, sending progress demos along the way so nothing arrives as a surprise at delivery.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Ship it — pick hosting and the extras a site needs, or take an app through App Store / Play Store review to go live.
